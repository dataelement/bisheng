## Design Context

### Users

Enterprise knowledge workers, application builders, and administrators use BiSheng to create, publish, govern, and collaborate on LLM applications, knowledge spaces, and content channels. They need dense configuration flows to remain predictable and efficient, especially when managing access and permissions.

### Brand Personality

Professional, restrained, and trustworthy. The interface should communicate enterprise readiness and operational confidence without feeling heavy or decorative.

### Aesthetic Direction

A clean, light, utilitarian enterprise workspace with clear hierarchy, compact controls, and blue/green theme support. Follow the tracked `@bisheng/ui` design specifications and tokens as the visual source of truth. Avoid ornamental effects, hard-coded brand colors, excessive motion, and visual patterns that compete with the user's task.

### Design Principles

1. Prefer established `@bisheng/ui` components and their documented interaction patterns over page-specific controls.
2. Keep configuration flows compact, legible, and consistent across knowledge spaces and channels.
3. Use semantic tokens so theme, dark-mode, and accessibility behavior remain coherent.
4. Preserve clear keyboard navigation, touch targets, and visible state changes for every interactive control.
5. Let permission state and action hierarchy carry the emphasis; decoration should stay quiet.
